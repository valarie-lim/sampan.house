The True Taste of Malaysia

From childhood memories to your table—experience the warmth of authentic Malaysian comfort food and modern fusion classics.

The Story Behind

The inspiration behind Sampan House started with a simple, comforting memory: crossing the scenic Sarawak River on a traditional sampan boat, eagerly anticipating the warm, soul-satisfying taste of a mother’s home-cooked meal. That deep longing for authentic flavors and family connection is what drove us to create a space where those memories could be shared with the people of Kuching.

The Passion for Tradition

Established with a passion for preservation and flavor, Sampan House brings "The Taste of Malaysia" straight to your table. We blend traditional roots with a cozy, modern aesthetic, making our space the perfect neighborhood spot to slow down, catch up with loved ones, or simply unwind over a peaceful coffee break.

What Makes Us Special

Our Culinary Roots

Our kitchen is guided by a Head Chef with over ten years of professional culinary experience, supported by a skilled team dedicated to the craft of Malaysian cooking.

Authentic Flavour

We believe great food starts from scratch. Our signature spice pastes, rich curries, and savory sauces are prepared entirely in-house using fresh, locally sourced ingredients.

Cozy Gathering Space

Designed as a sanctuary from the daily hustle, our restaurant features a calm, warm, and highly photogenic ambiance—ideal for casual lunches, family dinners, or special occasions.

Our Menu Highlights

Our kitchen serves up comforting Malaysian classics with careful attention to detail. From the perfect wok-hei in our Char Kuey Teow to our rich, deeply flavored Java Noodles (Mee Jawa), aromatic Ayam Goreng, and indulgent Lava Curry Toast, there is a comforting dish waiting for every member of the family.

Whether you are here for a full multi-course family dinner or a quick, quiet afternoon kaya toast and coffee, we treat every plate with the care it deserves.
